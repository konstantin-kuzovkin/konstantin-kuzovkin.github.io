About

I am a Senior / Lead System Analyst working on distributed, microservice-based systems in banking. My focus is solution and integration architecture: state ownership, idempotency, failure handling, reconciliation, API and event contracts.

Before IT I spent about 12 years in insurance claims, so I read financial processes from the business side first. That helps me ask the right questions early and explain technical trade-offs in plain language.

I work with REST/OpenAPI, SOAP/XML, Kafka/AsyncAPI, BPMN/Camunda, UML, ERD, data modelling and authentication/authorization. I am also building AI-assisted analysis tools based on LLMs, controlled tools and specialised agents, with source-grounded analysis and explicit hallucination controls.
